# Primary critical sources and proper domain boundaries

These are verified bibliographic leads from publisher, author, and journal pages. All operational rules in Coursecraft are **original engineering adaptations**, not quotations or exact mandates from these works. Verify access, bibliographic details, scope and editions when used in future teaching.

- Johanna Drucker (2011), 'Humanities Approaches to Graphical Display', *Digital Humanities Quarterly* 5(1): https://digitalhumanities.org/dhq/vol/5/1/000091/000091.html — graphics carry epistemic assumptions; capta as constructed; uncertainty and humanistic interpretation. Diagram-representation METHOD source.
- Catherine D'Ignazio & Lauren F. Klein (2020), *Data Feminism*, MIT Press open-access text https://data-feminism.mitpress.mit.edu/ ; 'The Numbers Don't Speak for Themselves' https://data-feminism.mitpress.mit.edu/pub/czq9dfs5/release/3 — categories, situated perspective, hidden labor and power in data. Epistemic/ethical METHOD source for visualizations.
- Hito Steyerl (2023), 'Mean Images', *New Left Review* 140/141: https://newleftreview.org/issues/ii140/articles/hito-steyerl-mean-images — statistical/probabilistic generative imagery and indexical questions; counter-pressure to synthetic documentary-looking diagrams. Art-theory METHOD source.
- Naomi Smith & Clare Southerton (2025 online), 'AI and Aesthetic Alienation: The Image and Creativity in Contemporary Culture', *Social Science Computer Review*: https://doi.org/10.1177/08944393251361449 — a specific critique of mass-produced AI visuals as aesthetic alienation; interpretation, not an empirically proven universal definition of 'AI slop'.
- Jernej Markelj & Jakko Kemper (2026), 'Doing the numbers: The operational aesthetics of AI slop', *European Journal of Cultural Studies*, published online August 1, 2026: https://doi.org/10.1177/13675494261468647 — analyzes slop as operational imagery aimed at engagement rather than communicative interpretation; apply as a critical lens, not a claim about every AI visual.
- Emily M. Bender, Timnit Gebru, Angelina McMillan-Major & Shmargaret Shmitchell (2021), 'On the Dangers of Stochastic Parrots', FAccT: https://doi.org/10.1145/3442188.3445922 — harms/limits of unchecked generative language-system development, data documentation, power/scale. Do not treat it as a benchmark validating course outcomes or diagram accuracy.

**Interpretive stance**: 'AI slop' is a contested colloquial/critical label, not a clinical quality metric. Distinguish (i) repetitive prose syntax, (ii) unsupported conceptual claims, (iii) aesthetically generic images, (iv) epistemically misleading diagrams, (v) poor user-agency design. A creative work can deliberately use repetition, a triad or an abstract image; functionality and provenance decide whether it should be criticized, not generic hostility to an aesthetic signature.
