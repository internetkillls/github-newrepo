# World-remap, state mapping, and adequate creativity

This reference supports the rule that a substantial local insight should be placed in the external historical/current world before being treated as a self-contained theory or design.

## 1. State-of-the-art as trajectory, not recent-paper list

Barry, Merkebu, and Varpio (2022) articulate state-of-the-art review as a synthesis of where knowledge is now, how it got there, and where it could go next. Use this as a METHOD warrant for trajectory-oriented frontier mapping, not as a universal review template.

- Erin S. Barry, Jerusalem Merkebu, & Lara Varpio. “State-of-the-art literature review methodology: A six-step approach for knowledge synthesis.” *Perspectives on Medical Education*, 2022. Open access / U.S. Government work with CC BY components.
- Locator: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9582072/
- Licensed operation: reconstruct current frontier through historically meaningful turning points and derive future directions.
- Forbidden promotion: do not assume a state-of-the-art review alone establishes consensus or causal truth.

## 2. Field mapping for software/research domains

Systematic mapping literature in software engineering supports classification of a broad field, identification of coverage, and detection of research gaps before a narrow synthesis.

- Petersen, Feldt, Mujtaba, & Mattsson (2008), “Systematic Mapping Studies in Software Engineering.”
- Petersen, Vakkalanka, & Kuzniarz (2015), “Guidelines for conducting systematic mapping studies in software engineering: An update.”
- Licensed operation: map topic coverage, publication loci, categories, and gaps before strong frontier/absence claims.
- Forbidden promotion: counts/frequencies do not by themselves establish quality, truth, or importance.

## 3. Absorptive capacity: external knowledge must be recognized and integrated

Cohen and Levinthal (1990) argue that innovative capability depends on recognizing the value of external information, assimilating it, and applying it, with prior related knowledge and diversity of expertise playing a role.

- Locator: https://www.jstor.org/stable/2393553
- Licensed operation: treat external knowledge acquisition and integration as part of creative/problem-solving capacity rather than optional citation decoration.
- Forbidden promotion: organizational absorptive capacity is not a literal cognitive model of an LLM or user.

## 4. Exploration and exploitation

March (1991) distinguishes exploration of new possibilities from exploitation of existing certainties and analyzes tradeoffs in allocating effort between them.

- James G. March, “Exploration and Exploitation in Organizational Learning,” *Organization Science* 2(1), 1991.
- Licensed operation: use separate exploration and exploitation passes when premature convergence or endless search threatens the task.
- Forbidden promotion: the organizational model is a heuristic for search-control, not a theorem about individual/model cognition.

## 5. Recombinant novelty and conventional grounding

Uzzi, Mukherjee, Stringer, and Jones (2013) found that high-impact science often combines a conventional knowledge base with an intrusion of atypical combinations.

- “Atypical Combinations and Scientific Impact,” *Science*, 2013.
- Public research summary: https://www.kellogg.northwestern.edu/academics-research/research/detail/2013/atypical-combinations-and-scientific-impact/
- Licensed operation: treat useful novelty as possible recombination of established and unusual components; search for both incumbent structure and non-obvious combinations.
- Forbidden promotion: citation impact is not a universal measure of creativity or truth.

## 6. Design-by-analogy and analogical distance

Chan, Fu, Schunn, Cagan, Wood, and Kotovsky (2011) report that far-field and less-common analogical examples can improve novelty/quality in engineering ideation under their studied conditions.

- “On the Effective Use of Design-by-Analogy...” ICED11.
- Locator: https://www.designsociety.org/download-publication/30665/
- Licensed operation: deliberately search structurally relevant far-field analogues when near-field search is fixated or exhausted.
- Forbidden promotion: distant analogy is not automatically useful; mapping and failure conditions still apply.

## 7. Arena taxonomy

The following is a controller synthesis, not a claim of one cited author:

```text
STATE OF ART      articulated frontier knowledge
STATE OF PRACTICE deployed/maintained competent practice
STATE OF DISCOURSE live community argument, pain, vocabulary, weak signals
STATE OF ECOSYSTEM releases, standards, platforms, institutions, markets, regulation
```

These states may diverge. Treat divergence itself as information.

Examples:
- research paper says a technique is promising while maintainers reject its operational cost;
- a product officially supports a feature while issue trackers show recurring breakage;
- a community has intense controversy before peer-reviewed literature stabilizes;
- regulation lags platform capability.

## 8. Community/social sources

Community sources (GitHub issues/discussions, Stack Overflow, Reddit, LinkedIn posts, social media, support forums, user reviews) are usually `DISCOVERY-SIGNAL` or practice/discourse evidence before they are general evidence.

They can legitimately establish, when sampled and scoped carefully:
- that a vocabulary is in live use;
- that a named complaint/workaround exists;
- that a particular user publicly reported an experience;
- that a community debate is occurring.

They do not by themselves establish:
- prevalence in the population;
- causal mechanisms;
- product-wide reliability;
- scientific consensus;
- normative legitimacy.

Promote those claims only through appropriate evidence.
