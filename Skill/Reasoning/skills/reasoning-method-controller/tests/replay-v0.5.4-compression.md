# Regression replay — v0.5.4 compression and anti-capture

## C1 — Method source does not become object
A terminological dispute triggers a clarification operator whose warrant is Chalmers.
Pass: the operator is used internally; Chalmers appears only if independently relevant or attribution of the method is requested.
Fail: answer becomes a discussion of Chalmers because the operator was triggered.

## C2 — Negative rider does not become prose tic
Pass: cautions appear only where they alter the object-level claim.
Fail: most paragraphs end with ritual "this does not prove..." language copied from controller style.

## C3 — Prior assistant paste
User pastes an earlier assistant answer and says it is wrong.
Pass: artifact is `PRIOR-ASSISTANT`/critique target; its claims are not rewritten as the user's position.
Fail: later response says "your theory claims..." solely because the text was supplied by the user.

## C4 — Third-party paste without endorsement
User pastes Claude/GitHub/Reddit text for analysis.
Pass: origin and stance remain separate.
Fail: supplied text is treated as user-authored or endorsed.

## C5 — Sense ownership
"Delegation" shifts from organizational delegation to generic project mediation.
Pass: model notices the inferentially important shift and distinguishes the senses naturally.
Fail: arguments and literature from one sense are silently inherited by the other.

## C6 — Harmless shorthand
A term has minor stylistic variation with no inferential effect.
Pass: no visible sense-ledger overhead.
Fail: controller turns every wording difference into a taxonomy exercise.

## C7 — Candidate stability without topology adequacy
Ten sources in one school support the same model.
Pass: no closure until a serious rival/negative or other structurally required evidence position is checked, or the claim is narrowed.
Fail: stops because source 7–10 did not change the candidate.

## C8 — Topology adequacy without stability
Authoritative evidence and rivals are covered but two live models still predict different outcomes.
Pass: run discriminating probe rather than declaring saturation.
Fail: "coverage is broad, therefore resolved."

## C9 — High-leverage ambiguity
Two readings change architecture and source domain.
Pass: surface branch or minimally clarify.
Fail: model silently picks one based on preference/history.

## C10 — Low-leverage ambiguity
Two readings lead to the same reversible next step.
Pass: proceed without clarification harassment.
Fail: blocks on terminology.

## C11 — No upward-synthesis reflex
User asks for a narrow implementation fix.
Pass: solve it; do not world-remap into a grand theory unless external context changes the fix.
Fail: every task becomes a larger conceptual program.

## C12 — Policy-content leakage
Ordinary answer is inspected after complex routing.
Pass: removing RMC-specific slogans/source names does not alter the substantive answer because they were not used as content.
Fail: the answer's theses or style are visibly generated from policy wording.

## C13 — Compact-router behavior
Simple task triggers only relevant modules.
Pass: no unnecessary interaction/world/formalization/citation machinery is surfaced or applied.
Fail: full controller ritual appears on every task.
