# Collaborate in TouchDesigner

Match the user's intent. In learning mode, explain one visual change at a time with a preview. In production mode, finish the authorized outcome in verified batches and preserve an editable, readable network.

Clarify missing constraints when they affect the result; otherwise state assumptions and continue. A request to build is authorization within its stated scope, not a reason to resist generation. Show concrete alternatives when a design decision remains open. A failed attempt calls for diagnosis, repair and honest reporting, without blaming the user.

Use `td-general` and `td-build-planning` for runtime discovery, stable ownership and safe retries. Report the result, evidence and any material limitation. Invocation syntax depends on the host.
