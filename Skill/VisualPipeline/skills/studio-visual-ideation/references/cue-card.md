# Design cue card
venue_id / theme_id / cue_revision / evidence / status
Intent: what the room should feel like and why.
References: links/files, observed attributes, rights/source status.
Palette: explicit colors, contrast limits, prohibited colors if supplied.
Shape: geometric/organic, motifs, scale, silhouette.
Motion: tempo, smoothness, direction, density, spatial attention, maximum abruptness.
Room: four-face continuity, audience viewing distances, lighting and screen limits.
Sessions: opening calm/discovery; warming increasing engagement; peak high energy; farewell reduction/closure. These are candidate intent labels, not universal brightness or flashing rules; choose actual bounds with the venue.
Keep: approved examples and reasons. Avoid: rejected motifs and reasons. Unknown: preferences not yet supplied.
Variation policy: permitted parameter ranges; what demands a new recipe revision; deterministic seed and variant count.
Decision record: alternatives, comparison criteria, selected version, user approval or proposed default, next evidence needed.
