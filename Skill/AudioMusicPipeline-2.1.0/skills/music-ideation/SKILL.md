# Music Ideation & Compositional Conception

Use this skill at the inception of a musical piece or audiovisual score to translate high-level themes, venue atmosphere, or graphic art into musical constraints: tonal center, tempo range, rhythmic density, harmonic palette, and visual score seeds.

## Core Procedures

1. **Aesthetic Parameter Bounding**:
   - Translate user's thematic brief into concrete musical boundaries:
     - **Tempo Range**: e.g., Downtempo (85-105 BPM), Deep Melodic (120-124 BPM), Drum & Bass (170-175 BPM).
     - **Tonal Center & Mode**: e.g., D Minor / Dorian for reflective, mystical energy; F# Major for uplifting resolution.
     - **Timbral Palette**: Organic acoustic instruments, analog synthesis, granular textures, metallic percussion.

2. **Visual Score Inception**:
   - For audiovisual works, sketch the **Visual Score Seed**:
     - Define continuous trajectory arcs (melodic line).
     - Define geometric pulses (kick/percussion anchors).
     - Define harmonic ribbons (chords/textures).
   - Format into `contracts/visual_score_schema.json`.

3. **Hand-Off**:
   - Forward scale and progression constraints to `harmony-theory`.
   - Forward metric grid to `rhythm-groove`.
   - Forward graphic trajectories to `audio-visual-crossmodal`.
