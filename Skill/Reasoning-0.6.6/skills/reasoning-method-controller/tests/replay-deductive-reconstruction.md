# Regression Replay — Deductive Reconstruction / Prose Surface

This test captures the v0.5.0 regression where the controller's bookkeeping leaked into the answer and systematic reconstruction became recap/ledger prose.

## R1 — "Reconstruct the theory behind this discussion"

Context:
The discussion contains delegation, agency, continuity, HCI, critical realism, posthumanism, AI governance, and several sources.

Pass:
- identifies a latent organizing problem rather than listing topics in conversation order;
- explains why the initial term may be too narrow;
- derives the broader object/domain before applications;
- distinguishes assumptions, major theses, minor consequences, and examples;
- makes inferential transitions explicit in prose;
- treats rival positions as stakes, not as name-drops;
- writes long connected paragraphs where appropriate;
- uses substantive headings, not controller headings;
- no `ROOT-SITUATION`, `LIVE-Q`, `JURISDICTION`, `CORPUS ROLE`, `t0`, `Exit:` or similar internal labels appear unless explicitly requested;
- source provenance is legible without becoming a source-role ledger;
- equations appear only after prose has established their interpretation;
- ends with substantive residual problems/research debts.

Fail:
- chronological recap;
- bullet/table-heavy answer where prose is secondary;
- visible controller state;
- headings named after stage/state fields;
- unexplained formula chains;
- a list of fields/authors that never becomes a corpus theory.

## R2 — User asks explicitly for controller state

Pass:
- internal labels may be shown because the user requested them;
- clearly distinguish controller state from the target theory itself.

## R3 — Concise answer

Pass:
- prose-first does not mean artificially long;
- if the user asks for concise output, preserve derivational coherence in a shorter form.

## R4 — Technical/coding task

Pass:
- do not force essay prose where code, diff, table, or command output is the appropriate artifact;
- surface firewall still prevents irrelevant epistemic labels from leaking.
