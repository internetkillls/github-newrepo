# Reference indexing, arena discovery, and evidence governance v0.5.6

## 1. Retrieval questions are distinct

```text
METHOD: which unresolved operation needs a warrant?
OBJECT/CONTEXT: which literature/artifact directly concerns the phenomenon?
EVIDENCE: what supports or defeats the live claim?
ARENA: what reveals current practice, discourse, or ecosystem constraints?
UMBRELLA: which external theory/pattern/tradition/regime explains the decomposed orientation structure?
```

Do not answer OBJECT/EVIDENCE/ARENA/UMBRELLA with METHOD sources merely because the method source is bundled or salient.

## 2. Roles are per use

```text
CORPUS ROLE: METHOD | OBJECT | CONTEXT | BRIDGE | USER-CORPUS | MODEL
INFERENTIAL ROLE: EVIDENCE | DEFEATER | ORIENTATION | DISCOVERY-SIGNAL | NONE
```

## 3. Arena-first discovery

When external placement matters, identify candidate fields, practices, repositories, products/markets, standards bodies, institutions/regulators, or discourse communities. Search their native, older, and rival vocabularies before importing philosophical analogues.

## 4. Orientation-signature discovery

When the task seeks a latent umbrella, do not search only the user's headline noun. Build search terms from the decomposed structure:
- governing problem/question;
- presuppositions/starting assumptions;
- explanatory relation or mechanism;
- characteristic argument move;
- accepted consequence/trade-off;
- excluded alternative;
- practical/institutional realization.

Run more than one formulation if a single vocabulary risks field lock-in. Treat structural match as a hypothesis to verify in sources, not as proof from search-result similarity.

## 5. Anchor-first attribution

Use verified anchor → identity/version → passage/artifact → attributed claim. Do not retrofit a remembered claim to a convenient source.

## 6. Version identity

Preserve preprint/final, early/mature theory, edition/translation, API/package/dataset/release state when it can change the claim.

## 7. Set-level adequacy and dual closure

Individually relevant sources can form an inadequate set. Audit coverage, nonredundant roles, dissent/negative evidence, version/temporal coverage, practice/runtime evidence when relevant, redundancy, and missing structural positions.

For umbrella mapping, include at least one serious rival or counterexample when the placement is consequential. If no candidate dominates structurally, keep plural placement rather than force a label.

Research closure requires both candidate stability and evidence-topology adequacy. Read `saturation-closure.md` when closure, saturation, novelty, consensus, absence, frontier, or exhaustive coverage is consequential.

## 8. Field tempo

Use `slow | mixed | fast | event-driven` only when recency matters. Distinguish repeated hits in one vocabulary from cross-arena coverage.

## 9. Creative search

For invention, search conventional precedents, unresolved pain/negative space, near-field alternatives, relevant far-field analogues, and failed/negative cases proportionally. Do not claim novelty without a novelty search appropriate to the arena.

## 10. Community sources

Issues, Reddit, forums, social media, reviews, and community posts may be strong discovery/practice/discourse signals and weak evidence for prevalence, causality, consensus, or general reliability. Promote claims only through appropriate warrant.

## 11. Tool failure

If retrieval fails, preserve the research debt; use memory only as unverified orientation; do not substitute METHOD sources for missing OBJECT/UMBRELLA evidence; do not claim novelty, absence, consensus, current state, exhaustive coverage, or settled umbrella placement.
